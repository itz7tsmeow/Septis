Controls & Engineering Shortcuts

  • Left-Click + Drag: Paint with Primary Element (for Fan, Laser, and Wind, your drag direction sets the beam/wind angle!).
  • Right-Click + Drag: Paint with Secondary Element (right-click any palette button to assign it).
  • Hold Shift + Drag (or select ╱ Line): Draw perfectly straight walls, metal wires, glass lenses, or fuses.
  • Fighter Control: Use WASD or Arrow Keys to walk/jump with spawned Fighters, and press E to make Fighters shoot your currently selected primary element!
  • Hotkeys: Space (Pause/Play), S (Step 1 Frame), C (Clear All), [ / ] or Mouse Wheel (Pen Size).

Advanced Chemistry & Crazy Contraption Combos

  • Electrical Detonators & Liquid Switches: Wire Metal tracks to C4 or Gunpowder. Use Mercury or Saltwater to bridge gaps in your circuit and strike one end with Thunder!
  • Laser Optics & Glass Forging: Strike Powder (Sand) with Thunder or Magma to vitrify it into Glass. Fire a Laser through Glass windows or bounce it off 45° Metal mirrors!
  • Nitroglycerin Impact Physics: Nitro is sensitive to kinetic shock—dropping heavy Stone or firing a Wind blast onto Nitro triggers a high-pressure detonation that shatters nearby Glass and Stone.
  • Thermobaric Fuel-Air Clouds: Fill an enclosed chamber with Gas, Oil, and Fireworks, then burn a slow Fuse through a pinhole to ignite the entire chamber.
  • Autonomous Biomes: Combine Cloud (overhead rain/snow), Seed, Vine, and Ants on a bed of Powder to watch self-sustaining forests and underground ant colonies evolve.
