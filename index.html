🔥 Thermal & Volcanic Reactions

Water + Magma crystallizes into solid Wall/Stone and releases rising Steam. Fire and Magma rapidly melt Ice and Snow into liquid Water, and ignite Wood, Plant, Seed, Powder, Gunpowder, and C4.

🌱 Botany, Acid & Contagion

Seed + Water germinates and grows branching Plant/Wood structures upward. Acid dissolves almost any material on contact except Wall and Clone while expending its potency. Virus creeps across adjacent materials, converting them into Purple Virus.

💥 Detonations, Clone & Wind

Gunpowder & C4 detonate with radial shockwaves, hurling nearby particles with X/Y velocity. Clone latches onto the first element that touches it and infinitely manufactures more. Wind imparts directional aerodynamic impulse across the grid.

Controls & Shortcuts

  • Left-Click + Drag: Paint with Primary Selected Element.
  • Right-Click + Drag: Paint with Secondary Element (defaults to Empty/Eraser; right-click any palette button to reassign).
  • Mouse Wheel or [ / ]: Adjust brush radius (1px to 20px).
  • Spacebar: Pause / Resume simulation.
  • S Key: Step forward 1 single frame while paused.
  • C Key: Clear entire canvas.

Key Element Interactions

  • Water + Magma: Magma cools into indestructible Stone/Wall while Water flashes into rising Steam.
  • Seed + Water: Seeds drink adjacent Water and sprout organic branching Wood/Plant trees upward.
  • Fire + Plant / Wood / Powder: Ignites and consumes fuel, generating flickering flames and rising smoke/steam.
  • Fire / Magma + Gunpowder / C4: Triggers violent exothermic explosions with radial velocity shockwaves (C4 has a massive blast radius).
  • Acid + Solids/Liquids: Dissolves materials on contact into empty space or gas wisps while reducing the Acid droplet's remaining potency; cannot dissolve Wall or Clone.
  • Clone: Remembers the first element that touches it and continuously outputs that element into adjacent open pixels.
  • Virus: Infects adjacent particles (except Wall and Clone) and transforms them into creeping Purple Virus; can be burned by Fire or dissolved by Acid.
  • Wind: Sweeps across the grid and transfers directional X/Y momentum to surrounding particles based on your mouse drag direction.
